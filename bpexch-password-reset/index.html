BPExch forgot password: back in, without losing the account

BPExch forgot password? It feels like a lost account. It is not — the balance, history and limits are all still sitting behind your ID, and a reset through the desk takes minutes. What matters is doing it in the right order and not making it worse with guesses.

Quick answer

Message the desk from the WhatsApp number you opened the account with, give your BPExch ID, and you will receive a temporary password. Sign in, change it to your own immediately, and never pay anyone a fee for a reset — recovery is free.

The reset, step by step

  1. Stop guessing. If you have tried three passwords, stop. Every further guess risks a lock and none of them is getting warmer.
  2. Message the desk from your number. Send your BPExch ID from the WhatsApp number you opened the account with. That number is how the desk confirms the account is yours.
  3. Receive the temporary password. The desk sets a one-time starter password. Sign in with it straight away — do not leave it sitting in a chat for a week.
  4. Set your own password immediately. Change it inside account settings before you do anything else, and do not reuse your email or wallet password. Then test a sign-out and sign-in once, so you know it took.

Locked out is not the same as forgotten

If you actually remember the password but the page will not take it, you may simply be locked: several wrong attempts trigger a temporary lock of about fifteen minutes. In that case the cure is patience, not a reset — wait it out, then sign in once with the password you trust. The full order of checks is on the BPExch login page. Reset when the password is genuinely gone; wait when it is probably right.

Choosing the next password properly

Most account theft in this world is not hacking — it is a password somebody could guess from your ID, your team or your city, typed into a look-alike page. Use a phrase you have never used anywhere else, at least ten characters, and store it offline: a notebook at home beats a note in the same phone that holds the account. Then the rule that protects more money than any password: the desk will never ask for it. Not for a reset, not to "release" a withdrawal, not ever. A reset changes your password for you — nobody needs the new one from your mouth. Lost the username itself as well? The BPExch ID recovery route starts from the number you opened with.

The unlock-fee scam

A message arrives: your account is “on hold”, pay Rs 1,000 to unlock it. This is the oldest trick in the niche. Accounts are never unlocked for a fee, resets are free, and the person messaging you is not the desk. Come back to this site, use the contact route you started with, and ask us directly.

After you are back in

Check the statement for anything you do not recognise, in case the lost password was not the only problem. Then fund or play as normal — a reset does not affect your balance, your open bets or your limits. If you are returning after a long break and the sign-in address itself looks unfamiliar, that is expected: addresses rotate, and the desk will confirm the current one. Questions we get asked about this

How long does a BPExch password reset take?

Minutes, once the desk confirms the request comes from the number on the account. Message with your ID, receive the temporary password, sign in and change it — most players are back in within ten minutes, match nights aside.

Does a password reset cost anything?

No. Resets are free and always have been. Anyone charging an 'unlock fee' or a 'reset fee' is running a scam — see the warning on this page.

I have forgotten both my ID and my password. Am I done?

No. The desk can trace the account from the WhatsApp number used to open it. Send a message from that number and say you have lost both; the ID comes back first, then the password reset follows.

Will resetting my password affect my balance or bets?

No. A reset changes only the password. Your balance, open bets, statement and limits are untouched — check the statement when you get back in, for your own peace of mind.

Can the desk see my new password?

No, and it should never need to. The desk sets a temporary password; what you change it to is visible to you alone. If anyone claiming to be support asks for your current password, end the conversation.
