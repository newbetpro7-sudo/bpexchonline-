Questions we get asked about this

How much does it cost to open a BPExch account here?

Nothing. The account and the ID are free — you only ever send money when you choose to fund the balance, and the smallest first deposit is Rs 500. If anyone charges you a fee just to create an ID, walk away; that is not how the desk model works.

How long does it take to get my login?

About two minutes once you message the desk with your name and the username you want. We create it, you sign in at zero balance to confirm it works, and only then do you think about depositing.

What is the minimum deposit and withdrawal on BPExch?

Deposits start at Rs 500 and withdrawals at Rs 1,000, through Easypaisa, JazzCash or bank transfer. Last month's median wallet payout was 38 minutes, measured; match nights are the busy exception.

Can I use the same account on the app and the website?

Yes — one username and password open the exchange on the web dashboard, in a phone browser and in the Android app. There is no second account for the casino either; it is the same balance everywhere.

Do I need a CNIC to open an account?

No. Opening an account needs a name and a WhatsApp number so we can reach you. We never ask for your CNIC, your wallet PIN or any one-time code — and you should never send those to anyone claiming to be a desk, including us.

Yes — the platform you may know as BetPro now runs under the BPExch name, and an older BetPro ID and a BPExch account are the same login. That is the only thing worth knowing about the name change; everything else about using the account is covered on this site.
