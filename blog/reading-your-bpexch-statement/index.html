Reading your BPExch statement: the four lines that matter

Your BPExch statement is the only version of events that never argues back. Every deposit, payout, bet and commission you will ever query is written there in four kinds of lines — and a player who can read them settles in one message what takes other players forty.

Published 21 October 2026 · Accounts · 6 min read

Line one: the money in

Every BPExch deposit you make appears as a credit line with the date, the amount and the rail it arrived on. This is the line to check first whenever a balance looks wrong: if a deposit you sent is not here, the credit never landed — and the TID from your wallet is the key that finds it. If it IS here and the balance still looks short, the answer is in one of the other three lines, guaranteed.

Line two: the bets

Matched bets appear when they are struck; settlements appear when the market closes. The two figures beginners confuse: your stake leaves the available balance the moment a bet matches (it has not been "taken", it is committed), and a lay bet commits its full liability, not just the backer's stake. A balance that "dropped by 3,000 on a 1,000 bet" is almost always a lay liability doing exactly what the confirmation screen said it would.

Line three: the money out

A BPExch withdrawal shows as a debit when the payout is processed, and your wallet's confirmation follows separately. The pair — statement debit plus wallet credit — is your receipt; screenshot both together whenever a payout matters. If the debit exists and the wallet credit does not, the money is in the rail between the two systems, and the desk can chase it with the payout reference. If neither exists, the request was never processed and belongs back in the queue conversation.

Line four: commission

On a market where you finish ahead, the platform takes its commission on the net winnings, and the line appears at settlement. It is the most misread line on the page: players compare the "won" figure on the bet slip with the credited figure and assume a deduction happened somewhere shady. The commission line IS the somewhere — dated, quantified and checkable against the market result. If your desk's panel shows a commission figure that does not match the market's net result, that is a legitimate question to raise, with this page open.

The ten-minute monthly reconcile

Once a month, do this arithmetic: opening balance, plus deposit lines, minus withdrawal lines, plus settled bet results, minus commission lines, equals today's balance. If it ties, your account is provably clean and you never have to trust anyone's summary again — including ours. If it does not tie, the gap is exactly one line of one type, and finding it takes minutes with the statement filtered by date. Send the desk that line — date, amount, type — and the conversation starts at the answer instead of the argument.

One habit makes all of this easier: never let a month pass with a line you cannot explain. Players discover statement mysteries from three months ago the way they discover roof leaks in monsoon. The statement is on your BPExch account the whole time — read it while the matches are still fresh enough to remember.
