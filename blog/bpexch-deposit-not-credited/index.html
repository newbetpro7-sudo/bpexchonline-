BPExch deposit not credited? Seven checks, in order

A BPExch deposit not credited is the most common scare in this niche: your wallet says the money left, your BPExch balance says it never arrived. Before anything else: do not send it again, and do not pay anyone to "trace" it. In several years of desk work, genuinely lost deposits are vanishingly rare — delayed, mislabelled and unmatched ones are an everyday event. Work these checks in order; most cases die at check two.

Published 27 October 2026 · Payments · 6 min read

1 · Has it actually been long enough?

Crediting is fast but it is not atomic. The transfer has to land in the receiving wallet, the desk has to match it, and a human confirms it. On a quiet afternoon that is minutes. At 8pm with Pakistan batting, every desk in the country is processing the same surge you are part of. Under an hour on a match night is weather, not a problem. Over an hour — or any length of time on a quiet day — deserves the next checks.

2 · Find the transaction ID before you message anyone

The TID is the single string of digits that ties your transfer to your account. It is in the wallet app's history and on the agent-shop receipt even if the SMS is long deleted. Players arrive in chat saying "500 bheja tha" — and the desk that evening has forty identical Rs 500 credits to match by hand. With the TID, your message jumps the queue because it can be answered in one look. Without it, bring the exact amount, the sending number and the time to the minute, and expect detective work.

3 · Check you sent it to the current details

Receiving numbers rotate. A transfer to last month's number is the most common cause of the long delays — the money exists, but it is sitting in a wallet nobody is watching for your deposit. If you used details from an old screenshot, say so in your first message. It changes the search completely, and desks recover these routinely; what they cannot do is guess that it happened.

4 · Was the sending wallet yours?

Deposits from a friend's wallet or a random agent's number get held for confirmation more often than any other kind, because the desk cannot assume the sender and the account holder are connected. It is the same reason withdrawals return to the funding source (the BPExch withdrawal page explains the theft this prevents). If someone else sent it for you, name them in your message and save everyone an hour.

5 · Look at the statement, not the chat

Open the account and read the statement before concluding anything. Credits sometimes land while the chat is still arguing about them, and the balance on the home screen can lag a refresh behind the statement. If the statement shows the credit line, you are done — the money was never missing, only the notification was.

6 · Ask the wallet whether IT is having a day

Both major wallets have outage windows, usually visible in the app or all over social media within minutes. A transfer confirmed by your wallet but not yet delivered to the receiver is the wallet's queue, not the desk's. Screenshotting the wallet's own status message and sending it with your TID turns "trust me" into a five-minute resolution.

7 · Escalate properly — once, completely

If an hour on a quiet day (or an evening on a match day) passes with checks 1–6 done, send one complete message: ID, amount, TID, sending number, time, and the screenshot. One complete message gets worked; seven fragments restart the conversation each time someone picks it up. And if the silence stretches past a day with money unaccounted for, that is a different category of problem — the dealer stopped replying guide covers what to do, in what order, and what never to do (pay a "recovery agent" is the headline).

The boring summary: deposits are a chain — wallet, receiving number, TID, desk, statement — and chains fail at links, not in the void. Find the link. The full funding routine, done in the order that avoids all seven checks, is on the BPExch deposit page.
