Privacy policy

Short, because our data life is short.

BPExchOnline is a service-desk website. We do not run accounts on this website, take payments through it, or ask you to register with it. Conversations happen on WhatsApp; the details you share there — your name, your chosen username, transaction IDs for deposits you announce — are used to provide the service you asked for and for nothing else.

We do not sell personal information. We do not ask for, and you must never send us, your account password, wallet PINs or one-time codes. This website itself sets no advertising trackers; standard server logs (IP address, browser, pages visited) are kept briefly for security and discarded.

If you want a conversation or a record removed, message the desk and say so. 18+ only: if you are under 18, this service is not for you and we will not open an account.
