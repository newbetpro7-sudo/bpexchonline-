BPExch ID: the username that runs everything

Your BPExch ID is the username the desk issues with your account — the name you sign in with on the web, in the app and at the casino. Small thing, permanent consequences: it cannot be renamed, so it is worth choosing like you mean it.

Quick answer

A BPExch ID is free, issued by the desk in about two minutes, and works as your single login for the exchange, casino and app. It is the same thing as your account — the ID is the name, the account is everything behind it.

Choosing an ID name that will not embarrass you in a year

Usernames on this exchange are forever. The desk sees the same patterns weekly: a phone number baked into the ID (shared with every screenshot you ever send), a favourite team spelt three ways, or a name so close to another player's that deposits get argued about. Keep it short, keep it yours, and keep your mobile number out of it. If your first choice is taken, the desk will offer the nearest variant — your name plus a city is the classic that still reads well in year three.

ID rules worth knowing

  • One ID per player is the sensible rule — your statement, limits and history live in one place.
  • The ID and password are both case-sensitive. "Ahmed07" and "ahmed07" are different sign-ins.
  • Nobody legitimate will ever ask for your ID's password — not the desk, not a "support agent", not a group admin.
  • If you forget the password, the ID itself is not lost: a BPExch password reset through the desk puts you back in.

Getting your ID

The route is the same as the account — because it is the account. The full walk-through, including the zero-balance test we insist on, is on the BPExch account page; the short version is: message the desk, pick the name, receive the login, test it, change the password, fund from Rs 500 when ready.

Lost, shared or locked IDs

Three different problems get called "my ID is not working", and they have three different fixes. A forgotten password is a reset. Five wrong guesses in a row is a temporary lock — stop trying, wait it out, then sign in once, carefully; the checks are listed on the BPExch login page. An ID you shared with a cousin who shared it with a group is a security problem: change the password first and tell the desk, in that order. What never helps is opening a second ID in a panic — you will split your money across two statements and confuse every withdrawal after it.

The screenshot habit

Players love sending screenshots of their balance. Every one of them shows your ID. Crop the top of the image before it goes to any group — half the 'hacked account' stories we hear start with a screenshot and a guessed password.

Questions we get asked about this

Is a BPExch ID different from a BPExch account?

No. The ID is the username; the account is the wallet, markets and history behind that username. One is issued with the other, free, and you never need — or should pay for — them separately.

How much does a BPExch ID cost?

Nothing. Desks issue IDs free because they earn on your play, not on sign-ups. A seller charging an 'ID fee' is inventing a charge.

Can I change my BPExch ID name later?

No — usernames are fixed once created. That is why the desk asks you to choose carefully rather than accepting the first suggestion. If the name truly has to change, the only route is a fresh account, and your balance and history do not follow automatically.

Someone else has my ID and password. What now?

Change the password immediately from inside the account, then message the desk and say the login was shared. Do not deposit until both are done. If you cannot sign in at all, the desk can force a reset after confirming you are the account holder.

I forgot my ID itself, not the password. Can it be recovered?

Yes — the desk can find your account from the WhatsApp number you opened it with. This is one more reason to open your ID through a number you actually control.
