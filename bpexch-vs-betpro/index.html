BPExch vs BetPro: what actually changed for you

BPExch vs BetPro, in one line: same platform, new name on the door. You do not need a history lecture — you need to know whether your old ID still works, why your bookmark died, and which app to install. Those three answers are this whole page.

Quick answer

Your existing BetPro ID and password still sign you in — same account, same balance. The platform now presents itself as BPExch, so login addresses and the app carry the new name. Nothing about your money moved.

The three things that changed

1 · The name on the door

Login screens, the app and new messages from desks say BPExch. The black "b" on the green circle — the logo at the top of this page — is the mark to look for on the sign-in screen.

2 · The addresses

Old bookmarks pointing at earlier login domains go stale. The current sign-in address comes from your desk; the page itself is still titled "BpExch Login" and still asks for just a username and password.

3 · Nothing else

Markets, your statement, your limits, your open bets: untouched. If a "new platform migration" asks you to deposit again to "move your balance", it is a scam wearing the rebrand as a costume.

What to do with an old BetPro ID

Use it. Sign in with the same details on the current address (the checks are on the BPExch login page), confirm the balance matches what you remember, and carry on. Only two situations call for the desk: you have forgotten the password (a free BPExch password reset), or the ID was opened through a dealer who has vanished — in which case read the dealer stopped replying guide before opening anything new, so your money's story stays straight.

New players: which name do you open?

Whichever name brought you here, the account you receive is the same account on the same platform. Open it once, through one desk, and ignore anyone offering a separate "BetPro account" and "BPExch account" as two products. Questions we get asked about this

Does my old BetPro ID still work on BPExch?

Yes. Same username, same password, same balance. The platform runs under the BPExch name now, but accounts were never re-issued — sign in on the current address and everything is where you left it.

Do I need to pay to migrate my account to BPExch?

No, and there is nothing to migrate. Anyone charging a migration or conversion fee is inventing one — the account simply continued under the new name.

Why does my saved BetPro login link not open?

Login addresses rotate and old ones go stale; that long predates the new name. Ask the desk for the current address and save the new one. Your account is unaffected.

Which app should I install now?

The current BPExch Android app, from the link your desk provides. Uninstall any old-branded build that stops signing you in — stale builds are the most common cause of 'wrong password' errors that are not.
