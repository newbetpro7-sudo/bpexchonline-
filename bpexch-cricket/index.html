Settlement rules worth knowing before you bet

  • Rain and DLS: match-odds bets settle on the official result, Duckworth-Lewis included. A match abandoned with no official result voids the market.
  • Sessions settle on what was played: a 20-over line in a match shortened to 14 overs cannot complete and is voided; completed blocks settle normally.
  • Dead heats and ties follow the exchange's published rules for that market — visible in the market's rules tab before you bet, and on your statement after.
  • Every settlement is a statement line. If a number ever looks wrong, the statement plus the market rules settle the argument — the desk works from the same lines you see.
