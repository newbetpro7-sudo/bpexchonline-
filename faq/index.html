BPExch FAQ: questions, answered straight

The BPExch FAQ — the questions the desk actually receives, with the numbers in them. Anything missing here, the contact page is one message away.

▶ ListenThis page, read out — the short version in under a minute.
How much does it cost to open a BPExch account?

Nothing. Accounts and IDs are free through a desk; you only send money when you fund the balance, from Rs 500.

How long until my account is live?

About two minutes from messaging the desk with your name and chosen username.

What is the minimum deposit?

Rs 500 by Easypaisa, JazzCash or bank transfer.

What is the minimum withdrawal?

Rs 1,000, paid to the same wallet or account you deposited from.

How long do withdrawals take?

Same day is the norm — a few hours on a quiet day. Full-time on a major Pakistan match is the slowest window.

Is a BPExch ID the same as a BPExch account?

Yes. The ID is the username; the account is everything behind it. One free login covers exchange, casino and app.

Do I need a CNIC to open an account?

No — a name and a WhatsApp number. Never send a CNIC, wallet PIN or OTP to anyone claiming to open an account for you.

Does my old BetPro ID still work?

Yes — same login, same balance, under the platform's BPExch name. See our BPExch vs BetPro page for what changed and what did not.

What is the BPExch login page address?

It rotates; use the address your desk gave you. The real page is titled “BpExch Login” and asks for a username and password only.

I forgot my password. What now?

Ask the desk for a free reset from the number you opened the account with. Never pay an 'unlock fee' — that is always a scam.

Is there a BPExch iPhone app?

No. iPhone players use the web dashboard, which is fully mobile. The Android app comes as an APK from the desk.

Can I become a dealer myself?

Possibly — through a master who sets your limits and commission. The account types page explains the tiers and what to ask before accepting one.

BPExch par account kaise khulta hai?

Desk ko WhatsApp par naam aur username bhejein, do minute mein free ID ban jata hai. Pehle zero balance par login test karein, phir Rs 500 se deposit karein.

Still stuck?

Login failures have their own page — the BPExch login checks solve most of them without the desk. Deposits that have not credited are covered in the deposit not credited guide. And if your problem is a person — a dealer who has gone quiet — read dealer stopped replying before you do anything expensive.
