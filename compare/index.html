Are BPExch accounts from different sites actually different?

The account, markets and odds are identical — one platform. What differs is the issuing desk: its fees (should be none), its payout discipline, its support hours and whether it observes basic safety rules.

Why do some sellers charge for an ID if it is free?

Because new players do not know it is free. Desks earn commission on play, not on opening accounts. An 'ID fee' or 'activation fee' is invented margin — treat it as a review of the seller, written by the seller.
