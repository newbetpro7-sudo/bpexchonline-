Betting odds calculator: back, lay, commission and green-up

A betting odds calculator that tells the whole truth: not just what a back bet wins, but what a lay actually risks, what commission takes, and the exact lay stake that greens you up on both sides. Type Pakistani shorthand if you like — "85" means 1.85 here, the way the bazaar quotes it.

Quick answer

Back profit = stake × (odds − 1), minus your commission on winnings. Lay liability = the same figure — that is what a layer pays if the outcome lands. Green-up lay stake = back stake × back odds ÷ current lay odds. The tools below do all three, live.

Questions we get asked about this

How do I read shorthand odds like “85” or “150”?

In Pakistani market shorthand, the number quoted is the profit on a 100 stake: “85” means decimal 1.85, “150” means 2.50. This calculator converts automatically whenever you type a number of 10 or more; type a decimal price (like 2.14) and it is used as-is.

Why does the lay liability equal the back profit?

Because they are the same money viewed from opposite chairs. When you lay a backer's Rs 1,000 at 2.14, you are promising to pay their Rs 1,140 profit if their selection wins — that promise is your liability.

What commission should I set in the calculator?

Set the commission your account actually runs at — the rate shown in your account panel. If you are just exploring, 5% is a fair working assumption; the point of the slider is that the honest figure is yours, not a default we chose for you.

Is the green-up figure exact?

The lay stake and both outcomes are exact before commission. Commission applies to the net winnings position at settlement, so treat a small green as a few rupees smaller in practice — the calculator shows its reading note whenever both sides land green or red.
